TASTE THE MAGIC

Welcome to the ultimate sugar rush. Explore curated rooms featuring larger-than-life confectionery art, immersive tasting stations, and vibrant pastel landscapes. Don't just look at the art - experience it, taste it, and make memories that are truly sweet.

BRING THE MAGIC HOME

Your journey through the Museum of Candy wouldn't be complete without a stop at our signature sweet shop. Fill a bag with our exclusive, artisan gumballs and retro classics so you can keep the sugar rush going long after you leave. Take a piece of the wonderland with you.

UNLEASH YOUR INNER CHILD

Leave the real world at the door and step into a wonderland of endless play. Whether you're navigating the twizzler maze or swinging on our peppermint swings, every exhibit invites you to touch, play, and rediscover the joy of our favorite childhood treats.
