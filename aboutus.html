ABOUT US

Step into the Museum of Candy, a sprawling, multi-sensory playground dedicated to the universal joy of a sugar rush. We have transformed the history and art of confections into a fully interactive experience, guiding visitors through exhibits that feature everything from cascading cocoa waterfalls and peppermint forests to hands-on flavor labs where you can engineer your own custom chocolate bars. More than just a vibrant backdrop, our museum is a deep dive into the global origins of your favorite treats, blending culinary science with unapologetic nostalgia. Whether you are navigating our life-sized gummy bear sanctuary or sampling rare international sweets in the tasting room, our mission is to awaken your inner child and remind you that life is simply better with a little sugar.
